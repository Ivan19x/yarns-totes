CUSTOM CROCHET WEBSITE - WEBSITE BRIEF

1. PURPOSE

Create a welcoming website where visitors can browse my friend's handmade crochet
work, explore designs and product types, and request a custom piece. The website
should help her show her work and receive order inquiries without accepting
online payments for now.

The maker can complete about three pieces at a time. This is an estimate, not a
promise of a fixed turnaround or a public order limit. The site must not display
the number of available slots. It should only show a simple status such as
"Slots available" or "Currently fully booked." The maker controls that status.

2. PEOPLE WHO USE THE WEBSITE

Visitor / customer:
- Browse crochet photos, designs, and types of pieces.
- Open a piece to see its description and whether it can be requested or used
  as inspiration for a custom order.
- Send an order inquiry with their own description and an optional reference
  picture.
- Receive confirmation that the request was sent and that the maker will
  follow up.

Maker / administrator:
- The site owner creates one private maker link and sends it directly to the
  maker; no separate maker login or customer accounts are required in the first
  version.
- Open the private link to access the dashboard. Treat it like a password:
  anyone with the link can manage the gallery, change availability, and view
  customer inquiries. Do not share it publicly.
- After opening the link, keep access in a secure browser session that expires
  automatically. The site owner can revoke access by replacing the link secret.
- Add, edit, publish, unpublish, and remove gallery items.
- Upload photos and add titles, descriptions, categories, and availability
  information for each item.
- Change the overall order status between "Slots available" and
  "Currently fully booked."
- Review customer requests, including their descriptions and reference images.
- Update a request's status and contact the customer to discuss details.

3. WEBSITE PAGES AND FEATURES

Home page:
- Introduce the maker and the handmade crochet business.
- Show a selection of recent or featured work.
- Display the current order status using words only. Do not show a slot count.
- Provide clear links to browse the gallery and submit a custom request.

Gallery / designs:
- Display crochet pieces in a photo-based grid that works on phones and
  computers.
- Allow visitors to browse by categories or types of pieces, if categories are
  configured (for example: clothing, accessories, toys, and home decor).
- Each item can have one or more photos, a title, a short description, and a
  status such as "Available to request," "Made to order," or "Inspiration."
- Do not imply that an example photo guarantees an exact match. Handmade items
  may vary.

Piece details:
- Show larger photos, the item's description, and any relevant notes.
- Offer a "Request this design" action that opens the request form with the
  chosen design attached to the inquiry.

Custom order request form:
- Ask for the customer's name and a way to contact them (email or phone).
- Ask what they would like made, including size, color, style, and other useful
  details.
- Allow an optional reference image upload.
- Allow the customer to link the request to a gallery design, when applicable.
- Explain that submitting the form is an inquiry, not a confirmed order, final
  price, or payment.
- If the maker has marked the site as fully booked, explain that new requests
  are not currently being accepted; do not allow submission as a normal order
  request.
- Show a clear success message after a request is submitted.

About / contact:
- Share a short introduction to the maker and how custom crochet requests work.
- Provide the maker's preferred contact or social links if she chooses to add
  them.

4. MAKER DASHBOARD AND ACCESS

The dashboard is private and accessible to people who have the owner-issued
maker link. The initial preview does not use a separate login or account
database. The private link is a bearer credential: anyone who receives it can
use the dashboard, so the owner sends it directly to the maker and replaces it
if it is lost or shared.

The dashboard should provide:

- A simple way to add a gallery item with photos, title, description, category,
  and request status.
- Controls to edit, publish/unpublish, or remove gallery items.
- An order-inquiry list showing submission date, customer contact details,
  request description, reference image, linked design, and inquiry status.
- Inquiry statuses such as "New," "In discussion," "Accepted," "Declined," and
  "Completed." These are for organizing requests and do not send a payment.
- A setting to show either "Slots available" or "Currently fully booked" on the
  public website. Never show a numeric number of slots.
- A way to view or download reference images and customer-provided details.
- A secure server-side dashboard API. The Supabase service-role key must never
  be sent to the browser or included in public website files.
- A short-lived, secure, HTTP-only browser session after the private link is
  opened. The link token should not be placed in a query string or sent as a
  page referrer.

5. CUSTOMER REQUEST FLOW

1. The visitor opens the website and sees the maker's work and current order
   status.
2. The visitor browses the gallery and opens a design, or starts a custom
   request directly.
3. The visitor enters their contact details and describes the requested piece.
4. The visitor may attach a reference picture. The site should explain that
   uploaded pictures are used only to understand the request.
5. The visitor reviews and submits the inquiry.
6. The site confirms receipt and explains that the maker will contact them to
   discuss feasibility, price, timing, and next steps.
7. The maker reviews the inquiry in the dashboard and contacts the customer.
8. The maker and customer agree on the design, price, and timing outside the
   website. The maker updates the inquiry status as needed.

Submitting an inquiry must not automatically mean the order is accepted.
There is no checkout or online payment in the initial version.

6. SITE OWNER SETUP AND DATA

- The site owner controls the GitHub repository, Vercel project, Supabase
  project, environment variables, and private maker link.
- Supabase stores gallery entries, order availability, customer inquiries, and
  uploaded images. Vercel hosts the public website and the private dashboard
  API.
- Customer inquiries do not require customer accounts. Keep inquiry contact
  information and reference images private to the dashboard.
- The maker link is temporary access for the private preview phase, not
  identity verification. Rotate the secret to revoke the link and existing
  sessions.
- The private link is for the preview phase. Once the maker has provided a
  preferred contact method, replace it with a one-time verification code sent
  to her verified email address or phone/messaging account.
- Verification codes are not part of the current private-link setup and must
  not be represented as active until a delivery service is configured and
  tested.

7. DESIGN AND ACCESSIBILITY

- Use a warm, creative visual style centered on purple gradients and white
  surfaces, with crochet photos as the main visual focus.
- Provide a dark theme in which light surfaces/text are adjusted to dark colors
  while remaining easy to read. Let visitors switch themes; remember their
  preference when practical.
- Make the layout responsive for mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
- Use readable text, visible focus states, descriptive image text, and
  accessible form labels and error messages.
- Keep the interface friendly and simple rather than overly technical.

8. IMPORTANT BEHAVIOR AND DATA HANDLING

- The public availability indicator is controlled by the maker and shows a
  status only, never a number of slots.
- Do not claim that a request is accepted until the maker confirms it.
- Validate required form fields and reasonable image file types and sizes.
- Keep the dashboard and customer inquiries private from public visitors.
- Handle customer contact details and uploaded reference images carefully;
  provide a short privacy notice explaining why they are collected and how the
  maker uses them.
- Show useful error messages if an upload or inquiry submission fails, and do
  not claim that a request was received unless it was successfully saved.

9. FIRST VERSION (MVP)

Include:
- Home page and introduction.
- Public crochet gallery with item details.
- Custom inquiry form with optional reference image upload.
- Private maker link and dashboard (preview access only; no separate login).
- Gallery management.
- Inquiry review and status updates.
- Maker-controlled availability message with no public slot count.
- Purple/white styling and a usable dark theme.

Do not include in the first version:
- Online payments or checkout.
- Automatic order acceptance.
- A public numeric slot count.
- Automated promises about exact completion dates.
- Customer registration or a separate maker login database.
- Automated email/SMS verification codes.

10. POSSIBLE FUTURE ADDITIONS

- Online payment after the maker and customer agree on the order.
- Email notifications for new inquiries and status changes.
- Search and additional gallery filters.
- Customer accounts or a private page to follow an inquiry.
- More detailed pricing, delivery, and order policies.
- Replace the shared maker link with a one-time code sent to the maker's
  verified email, phone, or chosen messaging channel.
